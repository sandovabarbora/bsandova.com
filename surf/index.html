Skill by lead day

When to book

A flight is worth booking when the probability that the day is surfable, given what the forecast says at that lead, exceeds the flight's cost divided by what a surf day is worth to you. Until the reliability table has enough pairs, the probability used is the base rate from the last 92 days, which is the honest prior and a low one.

Method, and what is a proxy

Forecasts: Open-Meteo Marine (Météo-France MFWAM, DWD GWAM, DWD EWAM) and Open-Meteo Forecast for 10 m wind, hourly, aggregated to daylight (06–20 UTC) maxima and means per target date. Truth: the same API's analysis for the past day, which is the final model state a surfer sees the evening before, not a buoy; the Portuguese buoys are available on request only, and this is stated rather than hidden. Because the forecast and the truth share a model family, the skill numbers will read optimistically; the model spread and the rank correlation are the more honest columns. Rule, v1: daily max swell 1.0–2.5 m, mean period ≥ 8 s, wind ≤ 25 km/h or offshore (E ± 60°).

Data. data.json, rebuilt daily by a GitHub Actions job from surf/data/forecasts/<date>.json and surf/data/analysis.json (collect.py, verify.py). Every number on this page is read from it.

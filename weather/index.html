Weather, checked.

The same question as the surf page, with a better answer key: the truth here is a thermometer and a rain gauge, not another model. Every morning this page stores the seven-day forecast from four weather models for two Prague stations, then scores each forecast against what the Czech Hydrometeorological Institute measured on that day, lead by lead. Temperature error in degrees, rain skill against the 1991–2020 climatology and against "same as yesterday", and the days the models disagree. Numbers appear when there are enough pairs to mean something.

Skill by lead

Mean absolute error of the daily maximum temperature per model and lead, in °C, with a block-bootstrap interval by ISO week; below it the two baselines any forecast has to beat, the climatological mean for the day and yesterday's observation. Rain: the share of the four models that call ≥ 1 mm, scored with the Brier score and shown as skill against climatology and persistence. Empty cells: fewer than 14 pairs; grey: fewer than 30.

Data quality, counted

Method

Forecasts: Open-Meteo, hourly 2 m temperature and precipitation for the station coordinates, models best_match, icon_seamless, ecmwf_ifs025, gfs_seamless, seven days ahead, fetched at 06:40 UTC. Observations: ČHMÚ open data, daily climatological values per station (TMA, TMI at 20:00 UTC; SRA at 06:00 UTC). Climatology: the station's own 1991–2020 record, ±15 days around the day of year. Verification: weather/verify.py, five tests over a synthetic world with planted error and bias; the same gates as the surf page. Everything is a public repository, a cron and static files; nothing costs anything and nothing is typed.

What changed after registration

  • 29 September 2026, choices the design left open: census reference dates come from national documentation, and several are marked for verification; a metro region's core is its densest member; the Czech rebuilt numerator uses monthly regional completions, and the within-Czechia comparison is the raw rate, with no model; lags are the best of 10 starting values; twelve quarterly lags leave 27 quarters, not 35, and the fixed-b critical value is simulated; the Polish bootstrap is the unstudentised score version, its effective number of clusters uses an independence working covariance, and the Polish common support is 242 districts; rail and tram distances use today's network; half-kilometre squares take their parent square's density; robustness bootstraps use 999 draws.
  • 29 September 2026, after estimation: H5 was decided at p = 0.0252 against 0.025 and not re-run. Prague's monthly noise was 3.3 times the signal, which triggers the registered rule that a failed pipeline test be written "underpowered"; the rule did not apply, because H2 passed. The rest of Czechia's 5-month lag and Prague's flat likelihood are flagged, and two pipeline variants had diverging bootstraps. Three relevance checks failed, and their results are labelled uninformative. Robustness variants for H4 drop district-builder cells with no dwellings at all, which the main frame did not have.
  • 29 September 2026, after the fact-check: two registered checks omitted from the first runs, the metro test clustered by the 57 city districts and the Warsaw replication, were added; neither is in the family.
  • Not run, because the inputs could not be obtained before the outcomes were joined: the land and planning layers for the metro question (Urban Atlas, heritage zones, flood plains, the 1999 plan and line D) and the analysis by basic settlement unit; Polish municipal construction times, because the statistics API refused connections; the stock of dwellings under construction, because the Prague series has not been published since 2009; one price-index variant (the Czech Statistical Office's realised-price indices of new and of existing flats) and the annual version of the price question; the exploratory analyses of the design's section 7, such as completions before and after 1 July 2024; and an OECD source beside the Doing Business figure, which was not verified in time.
  • 9 October 2026, clarification: the design's entries dated 29 September 2026 and headed "before any outcome was joined" first appear in the results commit 4e4301c, together with the results; their timing relative to the outcomes is the author's own record and is not separately timestamped.

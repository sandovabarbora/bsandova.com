  • 15 October 2026, reproducibility check and validation sample, before any attention share (overlap.py, validation_sample.py): the classification of the psp.cz records (379 034 speeches) agrees with ParlaCAP-CZ's label on 99.48 % of the 185 659 matched speeches of 2013–2023 (99.79 % without the chair), above the registered 90 %, so the extension is estimated. The validation sample settles three points the design left open: the strata are the ten topics, the other CAP topics together, Mix and Other (13, with equal expected numbers); chair speeches are outside the frame, as they are outside the analysis; and the coders see an anonymous key and the text cut at 1 500 words (111 of the 600 speeches are longer; the classifier itself reads only the beginning of a speech). The coding instructions give the CAP major topics with short definitions and “Other” for speeches without a policy topic.
  • 15 October 2026, validation coding: the two coders (Claude Sonnet and Claude Opus, each in twelve batches of 50, without the classifier's label) agree on 544 of the 600 speeches (90.7 %, unweighted); the 56 disagreements go to adjudication. A process slip: the coding agents wrote temporary files to a shared scratch folder, and two reported reading another batch's text there before re-extracting their own; each output file's keys match its batch in order, and the coders agree on 43–47 of 50 in every batch, with no batch out of line.
  • 15 October 2026, adjudication and validation results (adjudicate.py, validation.py, priorities-validation.json), a deviation: the author adjudicated 9 of the 56 disagreements and asked the assistant to decide the other 47 by her rule (“mostly borderline, so Other or whichever seemed more reasonable”), with her 9 as examples and without the classifier's label. The design had the author adjudicate (§2.2); the assistant is of the same model family as both coders, so the reference topic of those 47 is less independent. Weighted back by inverse inclusion probability: coders' agreement 86.4 % (90.7 % raw); macro-F1 over the ten topics 0.69 on ParlaCAP-CZ and 0.77 on psp.cz 2004–2013, above the registered 0.50, so no result is reported as classifier-limited. Weakest: unemployment (F1 0.33, recall 0.24, on ParlaCAP-CZ) and pensions (recall 0.47 and 0.41, word share 3.0 and 3.5 points too low). On the 9 speeches the author decided herself, the classifier's label matched hers on 3. The coding is fixed from here.
  • 15 October 2026, estimation code frozen under the signed tag priorities-code (tools/priorities/estimate.py, 34 worked examples on made-up data and a slow size simulation in test_estimate.py, reviewed by an independent reviewer before the freeze). Thirty choices the design left open, as implemented: (1) the wild cluster bootstrap is a score bootstrap with the null imposed, Webb weights, 9 999 draws, one-step re-estimation and a cluster-robust t with a leave-one-cluster-out (CR3-type) scaling, and for jackknifed models and Q3 the statistic is the same linear combination of the pieces' scores; (2) Q3's null is imposed by a minimum-distance projection of the two equations; (3) the randomisation permutes the regressor's topic series, centred within its spells, with a score statistic on y·exp(−β₀x), spells and fixed effects staying with the outcome topic, Q1r permuting the attention series, and only periods where every topic has the regressor used (EB103 drops out of the whole-series Q1 randomisation); (4) all 8! = 40 320 permutations for eight topics, 100 000 drawn for ten; (5) seeds 20261014 (bootstrap) and 20261015 (permutations); (6) intervals by test inversion, with outward steps to ±10, bisection to 10⁻⁴, a grid scan when the estimate itself is rejected, and a degenerate interval labelled underpowered when no value is accepted; (7) “the wider of the two intervals” implemented as their hull at each level (lowest lower bound, highest upper bound), so a label needs both methods, with each method's own interval and label stored; (8) “wider than 0.20” strict, “within ±0.10” inclusive, “wholly above” strict; (9) the split-panel jackknife of Dhaene and Jochmans (2015), overlapping halves for an odd number of periods; (10) the window after a wave is the 182 days after fieldwork ends, cut at the day before the next wave's fieldwork, and the window before is of the same length; (11) coverage is the share of a window's calendar days inside the corpus span (ParlaCAP EB81–98; whole series EB62–103, to the earlier of 31 December 2025 and the last date in the data); (12) half-year by wave parity; (13) dynamic Q1 uses all rows, its lag being a calendar window, and dynamic Q1r drops pairs across a spell change; (14) Q1r on Q1's waves; (15) yearly concern uses the waves of the spell of the year's last wave, floored before averaging, a wave's year being its fieldwork start; (16) zero attention floored at half a word, spending not floored; (17) Q2 static and dynamic, Q3 dynamic only in Q2's form; (18) Q4 party status only in ParlaCAP-CZ, a wave belonging to the government in office on most days of its window after (Sobotka 29 January 2014 – 12 December 2017, Babiš 13 December 2017 – 16 December 2021, Fiala 17 December 2021 – 14 December 2025); on ParlaCAP-CZ alone the Fiala government has 3 waves, not the six to eight the design expected (8 on the whole series); (19) checks get point estimates and bootstrap intervals only; (20) the Mix-kept check uses the study's own run of the classifier throughout; (21) the one-year window is 365 days, uncut; (22) “without 2020–2022” drops waves whose window before or after overlaps those years, and the list-change waves are 66, 71, 72, 77, 83, 93, 97, 103 and 104; (23) arm B in EB65, 71, 74–76 and 92, items only in arm B unused; (24) levels by OLS at the sample means, spending as change in share by OLS; (25) weights are mean concern; (26) the F1 ≥ 0.60 check and classifier-limited by the frame or frames of the corpus used; (27) the confusion correction spreads labelled words over reference topics with inverse-probability word weights, by frame; (28) Q2 controls by topic (log unemployment × unemployment, log old-age dependency × pensions; Eurostat une_rt_a and demo_pjanind added to the data); (29) a randomisation failure gives “not estimable”; (30) a failed check is recorded, not fatal. The size simulation found the wild bootstrap alone rejecting 11.5–15.5 % of the time at a nominal 10 % with ten clusters, CR3 scaling included; the randomisation interval's size was 4.6, 9.8 and 19.3 % at 5, 10 and 20 %, and the hull makes every label need both. A blinding note: while reading the validation code, the code agent printed the committed validation file, which holds the weighted validation sample's word shares per topic (about 600 speeches), not the attention series.
  • 15 October 2026, results (frozen code at priorities-code, priorities-results.json; no estimate failed): Q1 on ParlaCAP-CZ static +0.13 (hull 95 % +0.04 to +0.24), positive, possibly small, and dynamic +0.12 (90 % −0.10 to +0.22), underpowered, so model-dependent; Q1 on the whole series 2004–2025 static +0.18 (90 % +0.07 to +0.31, wider than 0.20) and dynamic +0.03, both underpowered; Q1r and Q3 underpowered on both corpora; Q2 (spending) static +0.03 (90 % −0.02 to +0.08) and dynamic −0.01 (90 % −0.06 to +0.02), not supported; Q4 coalition +0.14 and opposition +0.12, both positive, possibly small, and each government alone underpowered. No result is classifier-limited.

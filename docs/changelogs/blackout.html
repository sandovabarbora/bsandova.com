Changes after registration

  • 14 October 2026, registration: the design (docs/research/blackout-design.md) is registered by the signed annotated tag blackout-design, tag object 87ed802623d7d9c5e75f6d71e19f07951a45a92a, on commit d949b3de1348c4aed1c3c8e1a46036c440e4a7bb, pushed with the branch feature/BLACKOUT-1_power-outage before any record of 4 July 2025 was read on its own. Before it, a methods reviewer ran the draft rules on the ordinary Fridays only; those numbers are disclosed in the design.
  • 14 October 2026, screening (§3.1): trains were wrongly taken as a reference that does not depend on traction power; most Czech lines are electric. The first run, with buses and trains, flagged 29 ten-minute bins of 4 July, three as feed-suspect, and was seen. The rule was applied to buses alone, which flagged none.
  • 14 October 2026, code frozen: tests on made-up data showed that the "five of six bins" rule could open a run on a bin that fails; the opening bin must now pass. Fixed before any value of 4 July was computed.
  • 14 October 2026, results: the first run stopped writing its file on a NumPy boolean; bool() was added, which changes no value.
  • 14 October 2026, after the results, not registered (tools/blackout/posthoc.py): the registered median delay breaks down after the restart, because about half of the tram passes in 13:30–14:10 were matched to timetable trips other than the ones they ran; the share of passes more than five minutes off the timetable is added as a curve. A single time from it failed its own placebo and is not reported.

What changed after registration

  • 2 October 2026: two data definitions were added to the series design after the results of parts 2–5 were first computed, one for attendance figures that cover several venues and one for entries that count online viewers. Neither applies to any Bad Bunny entry.
  • 1 October 2026, after part 1's results were seen (post hoc): countries are also ranked by days alone, because small markets with many one-day number ones inflate the ratio. The registered ratio and its ranking are reported unchanged.
  • Added after registration and not in the design: the number of songs that make up half his streams, the count of songs above one percent, and the average price per tour. They are descriptive and labelled where they appear.

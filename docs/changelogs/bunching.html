Changes after registration

  • 8 October 2026, before screening: where the design contradicted itself on ties (§1 drops them, §4.4 resolves them by feed order), §1 was followed; a missing arrival is filled from the previous row only if the two agree within 5 s on at least 95 % of rows; the service date is taken from the trip's scheduled start; the terminal is added to each trip's stop list from its last row.
  • 8 October 2026, code, before any spacing was read: tests on made-up data showed that the registered estimate of amplification, a shuffled comparison, keeps the bias that recording noise puts into it, because the shuffle also separates each change from its noise. The primary estimate became the instrumental-variable one of Anderson and Hsiao, with the spacing two stops back as the instrument; the labels are unchanged and the shuffled comparison is reported as a check. The fixed effects are removed once and the bootstrap re-weights days; the shuffle check uses five shuffles. The rule for timing points, written against a planned dwell that is zero almost everywhere, marked nearly every stop and was replaced by one against the network's median observed dwell (65 of 624 tram stops). References were checked and added.
  • 8 October 2026, screening note: the share of trips on their line's usual stop sequence is 63–76 % by month, not 68–76 % as first written.
  • 8 October 2026, after the results and not registered: how pairs become bunched, instruments three and four stops back, intervals for the estimate between timing points and on departures, the persistence of bunches, the volatility of platforms and the places with most sudden closings, computed in tools/bunch/describe.py and reported as description, after an independent review of the results.
  • 8 October 2026, after an independent review of the article and not registered: line 9 and the Q2 ratio without it (1.72, 1.00 to 2.38), the power simulation for the stability test, the autocorrelation of the changes, the bus checks at deeper lags, and the pooling of swaps in the comparison with the shuffle, computed in tools/bunch/describe.py and describe_bus.py. The tram amplification interval falls under two rules of the design that disagree (wholly below zero, which the design lists as inconclusive; wholly within ±0.01, not supported); the margin rule was applied as coded and the article says so.

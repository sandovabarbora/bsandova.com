Changes after registration

All are dated in the design file. Before the data step: the Mirovision files end in 2022, so split votes for 2016–2023 come from Spijkervet, with Mirovision as a check, and the juror check covers 2016–2022. During the data step: the Wikipedia parser's header detection was made robust; country names in one source were mapped to codes; the rest-of-the-world vote was dropped from the EU test; pairs without a UN figure were dropped as registered, and a check counting them as zero was added. Before any estimate was seen: the registered pair fixed effects did not converge, so pair × contest effects, which contain them, were used. After the first estimates: a software problem in the two-stage estimator was worked around by dropping rows it cannot use, and the registered EU checks and the Brexit comparison, missing from the first code, were added, the EU checks with a stricter filter for usable rows than the main estimate; the check by audience for the EU test cannot be run, because no country joined between 2016 and 2025.

9 October 2026, correction: the article had said that no change after registration was made after its result was seen. Three changes came after the first estimates were printed: the row filter for the two-stage estimator, the registered finals-only and without-2004 checks (missing from the first code), and the exploratory Brexit comparison, as the design's note of 3 October records. On 5 October 2026 the design's status lines were rewritten in place; a dated note quoting the registered wording was appended to the design on 9 October 2026.

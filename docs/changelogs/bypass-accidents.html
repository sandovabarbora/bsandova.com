  • 16 October 2026, registration: the design (docs/research/bypass-design.md) and the list of bypasses (tools/bypass/bypasses.csv, sha256 485055ed073df50827a9443ae90f6cc33ec7b1b886a8e98cc1c8427909106033) are registered by the signed annotated tag bypass-design, tag object 58243948354418f313fbddf446cef0f8016b79e5, on commit c67ab09703c01c96bee089e0e642b7b0774f3984, before any accident count for a place or a year was computed.   • 16 October 2026, headline stage, before any accident count: the author decided not to request the CDV records of 2006–2015, so under §2 stage A is the headline, fixed on that day rather than on 31 March 2027, and stage B is not run.
  • 16 October 2026, data step and code, before any accident count by place: police record-level files exist in the same form from 2016 (2014 and 2015 only as yearbooks), so stage A takes works started from 2018; after the exclusions of §2, 35 treated towns (31 bypasses; 13 class I, 22 class II) and 50 comparison towns, with at least 15 comparison towns serving in every year. Choices made where the design was silent, after an independent code review: S-JTSK coordinates accepted within the national range widened by 20 km; boundary changes measured by whole cadastral areas (one town excluded at 3.7 %: Jičín, code 572659, which the design's change log names Valdice in error; Valdice, the other town of BYP-199, stays); Opočno's earlier partial stage sets its opening and start year; “two full bypasses” counts full rows of any class opened 2008–2025; a town with no injury accident anywhere in its municipality is dropped from Q1, Q2 and the outside change alike; separation in bootstrap draws is checked only on the indicators used; check 4 uses the year before opening as reference and check 8 splits the opening year by the opening month; check 10 and Q3 take the road the bypass took over to be the bypass's own number; the placebo interval is cluster-robust by bypass; Q3 length from the list and geometry from the listed OpenStreetMap ways; records of 2023–2025 without coordinates are matched to a unique RÚIAN municipality name. Code frozen under the signed tag bypass-code (commit 745023e7a4f836161f9bf27b4d8998f07746cd95).
  • 26 October 2026, after the code freeze, before any estimate was read: the registered run stopped twice without an estimate. The run command in estimate.py lacked geopandas and pyogrio, which the town step imports; and at bootstrap draw 500 the town-trend fit of check 9 failed (SVD did not converge) outside the guard the main fit already had, so the whole run stopped instead of leaving check 9 missing for that draw. The command now lists the packages and the trend fit sits inside the guard, with a test; nothing else changed. Code frozen again under the signed tag bypass-code-2, tag object 0ea21768754a990c2ff126438642fce28da49a2e, commit 6dce74f16668ff6d96ae753f52bf5ae4579460cc; the run restarted from draw 1 with the same seed, so the accepted draws are the same as before.

  • 26 October 2026, after the results, not registered: check 1 (all accidents, damage-only included) came out of the opposite sign to Q1, and the counts behind it were tabulated by place and year with tools/bypass/describe.py (docs/research/bypass-describe.json), which imports the frozen code and estimates nothing. The tabulation shows that Prague, a comparison town through BYP-057 (Úvaly), holds most of the comparison towns' accidents and that its recorded damage-only accidents fell by about half while those of the treated and other comparison towns did not. The counts are consistent with Prague being the main source of check 1's sign; this was not tested by re-estimating without Prague. The same tabulation shows Prague holding 83 % of the comparison towns' injury accidents, with a rise there against a small fall in the other comparison towns, which the article reports as a caveat to Q1. The article reports both as a note, with fig. 2. The share of draws behind each check and the fragility of the checks on killed or seriously injured and on smaller towns are pointed out in the article from the registered results file. No estimate was re-run and no label changes.

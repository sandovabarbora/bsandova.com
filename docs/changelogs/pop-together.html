  • The design reports β as exp(β) − 1 in percent; this page reports the same quantity as a multiple, exp(β): 2.9 times is +191 %.
  • 9 October 2026, after publication, following a model-based review: the registered coding of languages codes BTS's Dynamite, sung in English, as Korean, so the variable is relabelled "the language of the artist's market"; the registered check that codes it as English (3.7 times, 95 % CI 2.6–5.3) is now reported beside the main estimate (2.9 times, 2.1–4.0). No model or coding changed. Derived after publication, not registered: the interval of the days-of-income multiple (6.0 times, 4.5–7.9), computed as 10^(1 − θ) at the two ends of the elasticity's interval.
  • Added after an audit on 3 October 2026, not registered: the comparison of English and other songs' residuals across the countries that speak none of the five languages, in the section on Czechia.

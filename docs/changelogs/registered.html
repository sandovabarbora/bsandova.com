• 9 October 2026, version 2, after an independent model-based review: the definition now follows §6 of the editorial standard. The plan is committed on the author's branch before the analysis; the branch may have been pushed before or after the results, each article states which with its hashes and push time, and where it was pushed after, the order rests on self-recorded commit times. Prague, measured, part 2, whose design is in the same commit as its results, is named as not independently timestamped. "Usually pushed the same day" and "committed to a public repository" were withdrawn. Changes after the plan are kept in the design file and the article's change log, not in the article. The stricter rules for studies of named parties are stated as an intention, since no published study has used them. The reading rule now separates the margin of no effect, used for claims that can be read as equivalence, from the rule of the forecast checks, which have no margin.

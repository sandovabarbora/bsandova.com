Changes after registration

  • 8 October 2026, screening (dwell read alone): a departure, and so a dwell time, is recorded for about half of the tram passes, steady across months and hours; whether the weather changes that share was checked from counts alone, a check the design did not name (50.8 % of passes in rain hours, 50.6 % in dry hours). No stop has zero dwell, so the "stopped passes only" check coincides with the primary outcome and was dropped.
  • 8 October 2026, power (weather only): as registered, weekend and holiday hours 10:00–17:59 held 15 rain hours on 8 days, below the 10 days required, and only 3 weekend days and 1 weekday morning had both a rain and a dry hour in the window, which is all a comparison within the day can use. Before dwell was compared with the weather, optional hours were widened to 08:00–20:59 and hours are compared within the week instead of the day, as in part 2; the definitions of rain, the reading rule and the ±2.0 s margin did not change. The comparison within the day is reported as a check.
  • 8 October 2026, code: where the design left them open, the weekend indicator enters the model on its own (a holiday on a weekday shares its cells with ordinary weekdays), units are weighted by measured stops, the centre check uses the 46 tram stops inside Praha 1 and 2, the school-holiday check drops 17 April and 28 June – 31 August 2025 (Prague's spring breaks fell in February, outside the window), and the heat contrast uses weekday 15:00–18:59 against weekend 12:00–18:59.
  • 8 October 2026, series: registered as part 1 of a planned series, "Gehl, measured"; published as part 3 of Weather on the rails, which uses the same trams, weather and code. Nothing in the design or the results changed with the name.

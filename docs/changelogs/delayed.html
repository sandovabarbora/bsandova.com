  • 12 October 2026, version 4: the player was redesigned and gained a second engine, now the default; the first, disco engine is unchanged and remains selectable. Engine v2 sets the arrangement (sparse, full, lifted, extreme) by the surface anomaly z against the median day of the same type, the groove (straight, swung, half-time) by the share of stop events more than 90 s late (cut-offs 10 % and 25 %), harmony and timbre by z rungs at 0.5, 1, 2 and 3, the echo by hourly delay persistence over all 173 days, and the melody by the smoothed surface delay; its noise is seeded, so it is deterministic. After an independent review of this update found noise-sized z values in night windows with few events (the holidays lifted at 03:40–04:40, the Meltdown at 00:00–01:00, the typical Tuesday at 04:00 and around 23:10), the player gained a rule (commit 3ffc561), chosen after the fact and documented in its engine notes: a window with fewer than 1 000 surface stop events counts as a normal day (z = 0) for arrangement, harmony and timbre. A form layer followed (commit d5b2f67): the chorus is the bars whose smoothed surface delay is at or above the day's 75th percentile over windows with at least 1 000 surface stop events, with a two-bar pre-chorus, a drop bar, one fixed four-bar hook (1-3-5 | 4-3-2 | 3-4-5 | 4-3-2-1 in the day's mode), a tonic–dominant–relative–subdominant progression whose dominant slot follows the harmonic rung, and an arrangement one density state above the mood. The rule was prompted by the author's listening feedback that a chorus was missing; according to the engine notes its values were fixed before it was rendered.

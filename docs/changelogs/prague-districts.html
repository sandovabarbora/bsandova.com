What changed after registration

The author had worked with these data since May 2024, and the plan sets the analysis in advance. Among what she had seen before the plan was committed were the descriptive 2022–2024 budget figures of this page's first version, published the day before; about 35 lines of the 2019 city-grant list with their amounts, for 12 districts, inside the 2018 change's window; city-wide 2025 grant totals; and, through the design's reviewers, two districts' 2019 transfer figures. One 2025 grant total for one district was also seen while the lists were parsed, outside every test window. §0 of the design file records these. The analysis plan was committed at 683b0c8 and the first results committed at d8270ac; the changes below followed, all on 29 September 2026, and each is recorded with the runs before and after it in the design file11.

  • Choices the design left open were fixed: the denominator of the percentages, the exact form of the equivalence test, a simplified HonestDiD interval, a two-year placebo window, the set of parties in the party-level permutation, and the heterogeneity-robust estimator, implemented directly rather than with its authors' package.
  • The test window was cut. The city's lists change format in 2024, and the registered check of the two formats where both exist (2019–2023) found an agreement of 0.72 (0.80 after the corrections below), under the required 0.9, so the 2023 change is tested on 2023 alone. The simulation assumed three years after that change, and the test has one, with 23 switching districts against 17 controls, so power is weaker than simulated.
  • The 2023 amounts were corrected. The 2023 list prints its amounts in a column the parser did not read; the fix changed the 2023 total from 25 million to 4.1 billion crowns and moved H1 from +19 % (p = 0.21) to −38 %. The error was found because the 2023 total was implausible, not because of the direction of H1.
  • Flow direction and dating were corrected (commit 9b3ce92). A data audit found that the parser counted money districts paid the city as grants and dated some approvals to the wrong day of a session held over several days. With both corrected, H1 is −37 %.
  • The district-side covariate adjustment was triggered in the first run and not in the corrected one. Its trigger, a population-growth imbalance with p below 0.1, gave p = 0.098 with 1 999 draws and p = 0.11 with 9 999, and the adjusted estimate is shown either way. An imbalance in the share of children (p = 0.086) is not adjusted for, because the registered rule does not name it.
  • The first specification curve counted 18 specifications twice (Praha 1 is in neither comparison) and computed the bootstrap and clustered errors for only 18; the curve in fig. 4 has 36 unique specifications, each with all three.
  • The literature benchmark was corrected after the results. As registered, it gave Bracco et al. as 10–20 % and compared the power with premiums of 20–40 %; the Bracco figure was wrong, and the comparison is now with 19–47 %. As registered, the detectable premium was 2.5 to 5 times the benchmark; against the corrected one it is about two to five times.
  • The registered outcome counted money districts paid back to the city as negative grants. After the flow-direction correction, these returns are left out of the primary outcome, and the registered version is reported as a check (−36 %).
  • Reporting was added for switch-out, the always-aligned districts, the mid-term switches, the smallest attainable p and the HonestDiD interval at half the bound.
  • Two parts of the plan were not done: the alignment coding has not been checked against every constituent-session resolution, and four exploratory analyses were not run. Three analyses registered outside the confirmatory family were run but are only summarised here, with full results in districts_extended.json: capital-budget execution does not change with district size (H3, p = 0.98); 54 of 57 districts ended 2022–2025 with a better balance than budgeted, by a mean 5 970 crowns per resident a year (H4); and the city's 2024 formula change raised the allocations of districts unaligned in 2024 by a mean 18.8 % and of aligned ones by 12.3 % (descriptive, no test).
  • 9 October 2026, clarification: the "design's reviewers" named above as the route by which two districts' 2019 transfer figures were seen are the unnamed referees of the design file, whose reports cannot be verified by a reader; the article now states what was seen without a review claim.
  • 9 October 2026, correction of a registered label: H1 is reported as inconclusive, the label design §6.2 fixes for a null H1 when the ±20 % equivalence test does not reject (p = 0.53), in place of "not supported, underpowered".

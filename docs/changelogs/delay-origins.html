Changes after registration

  • 8 October 2026, screening: the thesis's column for the delay gained on a segment proved to be running time against the timetable, without the time at the stop. By the rule the design fixed for this case, the gain was recomputed as the delay at the next stop minus the delay at this one, so it includes the dwell at the segment's first stop; running time alone was added as a check before any value was read.
  • 8 October 2026, after the results and not registered: a comparison with traffic volume (the share of passes on the busiest tenth of segments) and the pattern of delay arriving at and leaving six large stops, computed in tools/late/describe.py and reported as description.

Changes after registration

  • 8 October 2026, screening: the thesis's column for the delay gained on a segment proved to be running time against the timetable, without the time at the stop. By the rule the design fixed for this case, the gain was recomputed as the delay at the next stop minus the delay at this one, so it includes the dwell at the segment's first stop; running time alone was added as a check before any value was read.
  • 8 October 2026, after the results and not registered: a comparison with tram volume, the concentration curve and its Gini, the overlap of the top tenth between odd and even weeks, peaks against middays on the top segments, the split of the gain into lateness made and early trams coming back to time, and the pattern at six large stops chosen after seeing the top list, computed in tools/late/describe.py and reported as description.
  • 8 October 2026, map: stop positions from the PID timetable in GTFS rather than the thesis's stop table; 599 of 618 segments placed.

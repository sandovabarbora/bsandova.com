What changed after registration

  • 1 October 2026: after the links were frozen, the share of applications linked to a release was 51 % for funded and 31 % for unfunded feature-length projects, low enough to suggest that changed titles were hiding released films. Before any estimate, a blind audit was added and registered (commit 3cbff57): 30 funded and 30 unfunded unmatched applications, drawn with a fixed seed, were searched by hand without their funding status. It found films released under another title for 3 funded and 2 unfunded applications, plus one unsure (e3871d6). The linking therefore found about 91 % of released funded projects and 82–87 % of released unfunded ones; the small gap would, if anything, inflate the effect. The registered response, giving the release estimate as a range that bounds linking errors (§6 of the design), was not computed; the "unsure" variant in the robustness table moves only the links judged unsure and is not that range.
  • 1 October 2026: a fourth linking rule, by production company, was added as registered with the audit. Wikidata names a production company for only 52 of the 999 LUMIERE films11, so it added 3 links, all for one project (508b49e).
  • 3 October 2026, found in an audit and not rerun: several registered steps were not carried out. Besides the linking bounds above, these are O3, European admissions; call fixed effects as a robustness check; the check on feature-length projects only; Q2 for each scoring criterion; a separate report of short-film calls; and the three-year release window for the 2016–2022 check, which used release by 30 September 2026 instead. Linking rule 2 was run without the registered condition of the same producer company; every rule-2 link was checked by hand. Five linked applications have 0 Czech admissions in LUMIERE and were still counted as released, against the plan's definition of a LUMIERE release; one of them lies inside the bandwidth on the funded side. Q2 was computed on all applications, not first applications only. The balance check of the producer's earlier funded applications was computed in alphabetical rather than time order.

Changes after registration

Each change is dated in the design file and was made before the estimate was run. (1) The author decided that the tram estimate is the only primary one and buses secondary, and confirmed the ±10 second margin (6 October). (2) The screening read PID announcements from the Internet Archive and was clarified in two ways: incidents do not exclude, and a closure in force on every date of the window does not exclude; the literal reading is reported as a check (8 October). (3) The records are stop passes rather than raw positions, the route is read from the trip id and the direction is the trip's terminal (8 October). (4) Choices the design left open were fixed with the frozen code: the bootstrap form, the placebo sample, how the nearest station is chosen, and that an empty dose band is listed rather than dropped (8 October). (5) A fault in the frozen code was found when it first ran on the data, during the power re-run and before any rain estimate: units alone in their route-hour cell or date broke the calculation of residuals, and they are now removed before every fit (8 October). One addition was made after the results: the post-hoc placebo, reported above and marked as such. None changes the rule for reading the result.

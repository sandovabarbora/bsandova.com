What changed after registration

  • 1 October 2026, after part 1's results were seen (post hoc): countries are also ranked by days alone, because small markets with many one-day number ones inflate the ratio. The registered ratio and its ranking are reported unchanged.
  • 2 October 2026, after the results were first computed: an attendance figure that covers several venues is a leg's total, not a run, and is left out of the run question. Three BTS entries of 2018 are leg totals; the parser flagged two (Los Angeles and Tokyo), and the third, Taoyuan, was flagged by hand on 3 October 2026 after an audit.
  • 2 October 2026, same day: an entry that counts online viewers with the hall is left out of the run and price questions. Three entries of Permission to Dance on Stage are affected. Both rules are in the series design with the reason.

Changes after registration

  • 8 October 2026, before any connection was scored: tram platforms have no parent station in the PID timetable data, so hubs are keyed by stop name, as the design provides; a trip's arrival at its last stop is taken from the last row's next arrival; a missing arrival is filled from the previous row when the two agree within 5 s on at least 95 % of rows; connections are kept up to ten minutes of slack; the period for counting missing trips is the day type, in or out of the summer timetable; the hour bands are defined; returns and same-street pairs are kept with a flag for the check without them; the screening's precision assumes an intraclass correlation of 0.01, since counts alone cannot give it.
  • 8 October 2026, code frozen with tests: the labels are applied in the rule's literal order, so an interval wholly below zero but within ±1 point would read "not supported"; same-day donors are the trips within an hour and 40 positions of the planned one; other-day donors are counted by week so each bootstrap draw uses donors from its own weeks; the checks are specified; references were checked and added.
  • 8 October 2026, five fixes for memory and speed only, after the freeze and before the results were committed, each meant to give the same numbers (Q1 equivalence was checked on the March 2025 rows, for the first three fixes together and for the bootstrap-by-week fix; the rest by reading the diffs): loading only the rows each analysis reads, running month by month and then each month in its own process, reading text keys as dictionaries, and summing the bootstrap by week before the draws (identical to within 4 × 10⁻¹⁶ on March 2025). In checking one of them, the March point estimate was seen before the full run.
  • 8 October 2026, after the results and not registered: what the uncertain connections consist of (unobserved, or flagged by a short hour) and how often they were made; the share of costly misses that followed one of part 1's busiest segments.
  • 8 October 2026, after the results and not registered, following an independent review: bounds that set only the connections whose planned tram was not observed to made or missed (the registered bounds also overwrote observed outcomes); the omitted §4.4 bounds for Q1b and Q2 and the §7 timing check for Q2; an out-of-sample test of the roulette's slack curve; the share of all connections after part 1's busiest segments, as a baseline for Q4; how far the departure stand-in sits from observed departures; the roulette's default cell, coverage and extrapolation flag.

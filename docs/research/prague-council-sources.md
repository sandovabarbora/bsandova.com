# Prague council study: sources for Appendix A

Collected on 28 Sep 2026. "[unverified]" marks points taken from a single secondary source.

## Coalitions

- **2010–14.** ODS + ČSSD: agreement 16 Nov 2010, Svoboda mayor from 30 Nov 2010.
  <https://zpravy.aktualne.cz/regiony/praha/ods-a-cssd-se-dohodly-na-vlade-v-praze-top-09-mimo-hru/r~i:article:683015/>
- **24 Nov 2011.** ODS + TOP 09 take office.
  <https://zpravy.aktualne.cz/regiony/praha/prahu-povede-ods-s-top-09-svoboda-zustane-primatorem/r~i:article:722712/>
- **23 May 2013.** Svoboda and two ODS councillors recalled.
  <https://cs.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rada_hlavního_města_Prahy>
- **20 Jun 2013.** Hudeček (TOP 09) elected with 36 votes; the council was a TOP 09 minority with ČSSD support.
  <https://www.irozhlas.cz/zpravy-domov/tomas-hudecek-z-top-09-se-stal-prazskym-primatorem_201306201338_mvydrova>
- **2014–18.** ANO + ČSSD + Trojkoalice: agreement 13 Nov 2014, Krnáčová mayor from 26 Nov 2014.
  <https://www.novinky.cz/clanek/domaci-koalicni-smlouva-v-praze-je-podepsana-stropnicky-bude-namestkem-254156>
- **22/23 Oct 2015.** Four council members recalled; coalition declared dead on 10 Nov 2015.
  <https://zpravy.aktualne.cz/regiony/praha/prazsti-zastupitele-odvolali-z-vedeni-hlavniho-mesta-ctyri-r/r~db2bb270793e11e594170025900fea04/>
  <https://www.denik.cz/z_domova/cssd-a-trojkoalice-prazska-koalice-je-jiz-definitivne-mrtva-20151110-jcbj.html>
- **28 Apr 2016.** Coalition renewed.
  <https://www.novinky.cz/domaci/clanek/prazska-koalice-se-po-pulrocni-krizi-dohodla-ze-bude-zase-pokracovat-349711>
- **2018–22.** Piráti + Praha sobě + Spojené síly pro Prahu: agreement 12 Nov 2018, Hřib mayor from 15 Nov 2018.
  <https://cs.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volby_do_Zastupitelstva_hlavního_města_Prahy_2018>
- **2022–26.** Caretaker council under Hřib until the SPOLU + Piráti + STAN agreement of 15 Feb 2023; council
  elected 16 Feb 2023.
  <https://ct24.ceskatelevize.cz/clanek/regiony/spolu-pirati-a-stan-podepsali-koalicni-smlouvu-na-prazskem-magistratu-9872>
- **17 Feb 2023.** Kordová Marvanová expelled from the SPOLU club.
  <https://www.irozhlas.cz/zpravy-domov/praha-zastupitelstvo-spolu-marvanova-klub-vylouceni_2302171618_ank>
  [unverified URL form; reported by iROZHLAS 17 Feb 2023]
- **June 2026.** Komrsková leaves the Pirates for "Jsme Team", keeping her post as deputy mayor.
  <https://prazsky.denik.cz/komunalni-volby/pirati-proti-piratum-komrskova-odchazi-k-rivalum-funkci-si-necha/>

## Rules of procedure and equipment

- **Rules of procedure.** Consolidated text:
  <https://praha.eu/documents/d/praha/jednaci_rad_zastupitelstva_hlavniho_2048941>. Amendments were adopted by
  resolutions 7/8 (2011), 12/75 (2011), 13/2 (2012), 7/1 (2015), 19/101 (2016), 4/48 (2019), 19/2 (2020),
  25/1 (2021), 28/5 (2025) and 31/41 (2026).
  - The 2019 amendment is described in the stenographic record of 28 Feb 2019 as "převážně technického charakteru".
  - The 2021 amendment introduced videoconference participation, with cards, for members in quarantine.
- **Chair's wording.** Unchanged ("Kdo je pro? Proti? Zdržel se?") in the stenographic records of 2015, 2016, 2019,
  2021 and 2023.
- **Voting equipment.** New equipment was tendered in 2023:
  <https://www.prazskypatriot.cz/magistrat-opravi-vybaveni-zastupitelskeho-salu-a-kabely-v-budove/>

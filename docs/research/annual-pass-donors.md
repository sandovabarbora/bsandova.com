# Annual-pass study — donor screening (§3)

**Date: 6 October 2026. Written before any passenger value was read.** Screening used only price histories, tariff
documents, methodological notes, network and organisation histories, and the years each official series covers
(report listings, table headers). No passenger value, trend or ratio was read for this file, with one disclosed
exception below that was not recorded or used.

## How the rules were applied

- **Rule 1** (official annual series for every year of the window, one source, no documented counting change). If no
  single official series for every year could be confirmed, the city is out as "not verifiable".
- **Rule 2** (no cut of 10 % or more in the annual pass, no fare-free or flat-rate regional pass for it). The pass is
  the full-fare annual pass for the city zone. Where a city has no annual pass, the longest season pass is used. A
  city or resident subsidy that lowers the price counts as a cut. National concession schemes that leave the adult
  pass unchanged are flagged but do not exclude.
- **Rule 3** (no documented merger or extension into a new area). This is read for **the network the outcome series
  counts**: the city operator's own network. That matches §2, which uses DPP only and not the PID region, and §6,
  which treats regional growth as a sensitivity. Growth of a regional integrated tariff area that does not change the
  operator's own network does not exclude, but it is noted. An operator's own lines into a new municipality or country
  do exclude.
- **Shared Czech shock.** The national 75 % concession discount for children, students and seniors from 1 September
  2018 applies to every Czech city in the Prague window. It is noted, not used to exclude.

## Disclosures

- Once, during the Brno check, an unmasked text search of DPMB's 2012 annual report printed one sentence giving the
  direction of that year's change in Brno passengers. It was not recorded, reported or used, and it does not affect
  any rule above: Brno's decision rests only on report coverage, price lists and network history.
- Sources unreachable during screening: GUS BDL, Munich open data (503), the Destatis GENESIS guest login, and at times
  the Wayback search index and the Wikipedia API. Several "not verifiable" verdicts (Munich, Berlin, Hamburg, Warsaw,
  Budapest, Bratislava, Plzeň) could change if an official series covering the whole window is found. Any such change
  would be a dated deviation, made before outcomes are read.

## Donor pools

- **Vienna (2004–2016): Prague (window cut to 2004–2014) and Brno. Count: 2.**
- **Prague (2008–2019): Brno. Count: 1.**

Both pools have fewer than four donors. Under §3, **both events are reported as not estimable** as pre-specified. Any
change to the rules or the city list made to reach four donors would be a deviation, recorded under "Changes after
registration" in the design, with its date and reason, before any passenger value is read.

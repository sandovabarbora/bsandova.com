Running late

Half of the tram delay builds up on a tenth of the track, trams of a line barely close in on each other (inconclusive), and connecting lines share delays only slightly, against expectations.

Overwhelmed? Here's the short version
  • Each part fixes its question, its measure and its reading rule before the data that answers it is read.
  • Part 1: over 21.4 million tram passes on 618 segments, a tenth of the segments record 50.6 % of all delay gained, just over the half set in advance, so concentration is supported, narrowly; they are the same segments in odd and even weeks.
  • Many of the largest totals are segments leaving large stops, and part of what they record is early trams waiting for their time; both were described afterwards, not registered.
  • Part 2: over 243 465 pairs of trams of the same line, the spacing between them shows practically no amplification from stop to stop (−0.0012 per stop, inconclusive by the registered rule); bunches, 0.43 % of pair passes and a floor, build gradually towards the end of a line, and the rare sudden closings concentrate partly by line. The parts

  1. Part 1 · where delay appears · 618 tram segments, 15 March – 8 September 2025

    Where do Prague's trams fall behind?

    A tenth of the segments record 50.6 % of all delay gained, just over half, and they are the same ones in odd and even weeks. Many of them leave large stops, and part of what they record is early trams waiting for their time, both described afterwards and not registered.

  2. Part 2 · bunching · 243 465 pairs of trams, 15 March – 8 September 2025

    How often do two trams of a line arrive together?

    The spacing between two trams of a line shows practically no amplification from stop to stop (−0.0012 per stop, inconclusive by the registered rule, corrected in its version 2), and bunches build gradually towards the end of a line. The rare sudden closings concentrate on some platforms, as other rare one-stop events do, and partly by line. City buses show a small positive value (+0.0021, supported), about the size of the spread among the tram estimates.

  3. Part 3 · transfers · tram-to-tram connections, 15 March – 8 September 2025

    Will you make your tram connection?

    Planned three minutes apart, a connection was made 80 times in a hundred and cost over five minutes more 22 % of the time. The two lines' delays go together only slightly, about +0.6 percentage points (+0.64 registered, not supported, and +0.1 to +1.2 across specifications), with real differences between pairs of lines and no mechanism identified. A roulette gives the share made for 6 624 combinations of stop, lines and time of day.

  4. Part 4 · four networks · Zurich, Basel and Bern, 15 March – 8 September 2025

    Do Swiss trams pile up delay like Prague's?

    On Swiss trams over the same season, the same measure finds a tenth of the segments making 39.9 % of the delay in Zurich, 51.6 % in Basel and 48.2 % in Bern. Basel and Bern are like Prague by the registered rule and Zurich is not. Above what chance alone would give, all three concentrate less than Prague.

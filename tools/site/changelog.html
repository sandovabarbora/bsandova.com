What changed, and when.

The status page says whether the jobs ran. This page says what the site itself did: every commit, its area, the files it touched, and the commit to read if you want the diff. Job commits, the surf collector and the model watch writing their data, are kept and greyed, so the two kinds of change stay distinguishable. The commit list is not typed; it is rebuilt from git log on every deploy. Above it are the articles' own change logs, written by hand: for the registered studies, every change made after registration, then corrections, version notes and the editorial-standard check, each under its article, which links here from its version number.

Theses

Three university theses, on transit delays, a university's logs and EU votes.

  • How long does a Prague delay linger?A sudden delay on Prague's buses and trams is still half there after about 33 minutes, and seeing it coming is worth about 204 CZK a year by bus on a model index.ThesisJul 2026
  • Can one pipeline read all of a university's logs?A master's thesis turned log lines into patterns and, on one hand-labelled test, scored F1 96.0 % against 79.1 % for hand-written rules, as reported, a figure that still needs a fair re-test.ThesisMay 2025
  • Did France and Germany vote closer after Aachen?In the EU Council their votes did not move closer after the 2019 treaty, and the other shifts the bachelor's thesis found cannot be credited to it.ThesisApr 2021

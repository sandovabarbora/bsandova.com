About

Data and cloud engineer, Prague

Barbora Šandová is a data and cloud engineer at Alma Career in Prague, where she works on a cross-cloud data platform and on LLM assistants over its warehouse. Her research interests are the applied econometrics of urban systems and the evaluation of LLM systems that answer questions over data. Her master's thesis on the persistence of public transport delays in Prague received the IES Director's Award for Distinguished Theses at Charles University in 2026.How the studies here are planned: what “pre-specified” means
